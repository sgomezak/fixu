# Madrid public holidays

One file per year. Every entry names the official bulletin it comes from.

- **national / regional:** BOE resolution of the Dirección General de Trabajo (Madrid column), cross-checked with the Comunidad de Madrid decree in the BOCM.
- **local (Madrid city):** BOCM resolution of local holidays for the year.
- `provisional: true` means the date was announced but its official bulletin is not out yet. **To do:** when the BOCM publishes the 2027 local-holiday resolution (usually early December 2026), confirm Madrid's two dates, add the URL and drop `provisional`.
